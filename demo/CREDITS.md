# Demo home

The demo home, its cameras, the clips and the reference photos are AI-generated for this project. No real place, person or animal is shown, and the names (Luna, Anna, the cameras) are made up.

- Stills of the home and of what happens in it: image generation of OpenAI's Codex CLI, edited from one base view per camera so the framing stays the same.
- Clips: Wan 2.2 (image to video, first and last frame), run locally; then the camera's time and name burned in.
- The reference photos of Luna and Anna are crops of the stills.

The clips and photos are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The AI answers in `demo.yaml` are recorded from one real run of the app with Gemini.
